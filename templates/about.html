Our Mission

The Camden Health Equity Data Visualization Initiative (CHEDVI) is dedicated to addressing health disparities through innovative data visualization and community-centered research.

We believe that by making health data accessible and understandable, we can empower communities, inform policy decisions, and drive meaningful change in health outcomes across Camden, New Jersey.

The Challenge

Understanding Camden's Health Disparities

Health Disparities

Significant differences in health outcomes exist across Camden neighborhoods, with some areas experiencing disproportionately high rates of chronic diseases.

Data Fragmentation

Health data is often scattered across multiple sources and formats, making it difficult for communities and policymakers to access and understand.

Community Engagement

Limited tools exist for community members to explore and understand health data relevant to their neighborhoods and daily lives.
