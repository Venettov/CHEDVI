Health Outcomes Overview

Comparative analysis of key health indicators across all neighborhoods.

Diabetes Prevalence

Comparing diabetes rates across all Camden neighborhoods.

Why Monitor This?

High diabetes rates are often linked to food access and economic factors. Identifying hotspots allows for targeted screening and nutritional support programs.

Obesity Rates

Distribution of obesity percentages by neighborhood.

Why Monitor This?

Obesity is a leading risk factor for other chronic conditions. Areas with high rates may lack walkable infrastructure or access to affordable fresh foods.

Asthma Cases

Respiratory health indicators across the city.

Why Monitor This?

Asthma rates often correlate with environmental factors like housing quality (mold/dust) and proximity to industrial pollutants or heavy traffic.

Mental Distress

Self-reported frequent mental distress rates.

Why Monitor This?

Mental health is deeply connected to social stability. High rates of distress may indicate a need for greater community support services and economic relief.
