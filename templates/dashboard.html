Understanding Camden's Health Disparities

What the data reveals about health equity across neighborhoods

Critical Disparities

Bergen Square shows the most concerning health profile with the lowest income ($12,104) and highest poverty rate (54.4%), demonstrating urgent need for intervention.

  • • 4.9x income gap between neighborhoods
  • • 42.5% poverty rate disparity
  • • Strong correlation between income and health
Success Stories

Beideman demonstrates what's possible with the highest income ($58,983) and lowest diabetes rate (13.4%), serving as a model for other neighborhoods.

  • • 13.4% diabetes rate (lowest in Camden)
  • • 11.9% poverty rate (lowest in Camden)
  • • Economic stability enables better health
Policy Implications

The data clearly shows that economic interventions could significantly improve health outcomes across Camden's most vulnerable neighborhoods.

  • • Target poverty reduction programs
  • • Expand healthcare access in high-need areas
  • • Address social determinants systematically
