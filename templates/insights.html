Advanced Health Equity Analytics

Comprehensive insights for deeper understanding and targeted interventions

1. Temporal Trends & Disparities Over Time
Analysis:

Health disparities between Camden's highest and lowest performing neighborhoods have widened by 15% over the past 5 years, with diabetes rates showing the most significant divergence.

Impact:

Early intervention programs in vulnerable neighborhoods could prevent this trend from continuing.

2. Spatial Analysis & Geographic Patterns
Analysis:

Health outcomes cluster geographically - neighborhoods within 1 mile of each other show similar diabetes rates (correlation 0.72), indicating shared environmental factors.

Impact:

Regional interventions targeting multiple adjacent neighborhoods could be more effective than isolated programs.
