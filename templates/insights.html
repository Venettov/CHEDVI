Housing Quality Correlations

Insurance Coverage Gaps
Why This Matters:

Uninsured rates dictate a neighborhood's ability to access preventative screenings and early intervention.

A higher percentage of uninsured residents often correlates with higher emergency room dependency for basic care.

Socioeconomic Stress Patterns
Why This Matters:

Persistent economic insecurity leads to "Toxic Stress," impacting both mental health and cardiovascular systems.

Bubble size indicates Unemployment Rate, showing the cumulative effect of multi-dimensional poverty.

Strategic Insights for Camden
Data Evidence:
  • Variable Outcomes: Significant health gaps exist across Camden's 19 neighborhoods, suggesting localized rather than city-wide interventions are needed.
  • Resource Impact: Proximity to food and healthcare access shows a direct mathematical correlation with chronic disease prevalence.
Priority Actions:
  • Precision Allocation: Direct public health funding to the specific tracts identified in the "Housing" and "Stress" charts.
  • Coalition Building: Engage neighborhood leaders in Beideman and Liberty Park to model peer-to-peer health support networks.
